Memecoin Tycoon
Privacy Policy & Support

Last updated: November 23, 2026

The short version

  • Memecoin Tycoon is a game. Every coin, price, wallet and person in it is fictional. There is no real money, cryptocurrency, wallet connection or trading, and nothing can be bought or cashed out.
  • There are no accounts and no sign-in. Your progress is saved only on your device.
  • The global leaderboard is optional. If you join it, your chosen username and best in-game score are stored on our server, and you can delete them anytime.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which may collect device information to show and measure ads.
  • We don't collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location, and we never sell personal information.

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (in-game cash, upgrades, outfits, stats and settings such as sound on/off) is saved locally on your device using the operating system's standard app storage. We never receive it. Deleting the app, or using More → Reset all progress, deletes it permanently. Because it never leaves your device, we can't restore it for you.

If you allow notifications, the game schedules local reminders on your device (for example "your stake paid out"). They are created on the phone itself, and no notification data is sent to us or anyone else. You can turn them off anytime in your phone's settings.

2. Sharing and reviews

The Flex button turns a winning trade into a picture you can share. The picture is made on your phone and handed to your phone's own share sheet. It goes only where you choose to send it, and nothing is sent to us.

Now and then the game may ask you to rate it using Apple's or Google's built-in review prompt. That prompt is run by Apple or Google, and we don't receive anything from it except the public review you choose to write.

3. Global leaderboard (optional)

The leaderboard is off until you join it from More → Global leaderboard. If you join, we store on our leaderboard server (hosted by Supabase, Inc.):

  • the username you choose, shown publicly to all players;
  • your best in-game net worth, Wisdom level and platform (iOS or Android);
  • a random anonymous ID created for this installation (no email, name or phone number);
  • the time of your last update, and any reports you make about offensive names.

We use this only to show the leaderboard and to moderate offensive names. Usernames go through a word filter, and you can report or hide any name from the leaderboard screen. We may rename or remove entries that are offensive or clearly cheated.

Deleting your data: tap More → Remove me from the leaderboard. That deletes your username, score and anonymous ID from the server. Reset all progress deletes them as well.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Full-screen ads appear only at natural breaks (when switching tabs, at most once every few minutes), and rewarded ads play only when you choose to watch one for a bonus.

To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may collect and process:

  • device identifiers such as the advertising ID (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android);
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it, device model, operating system version and language;
  • ad interaction data (for example, that an ad was shown or tapped), and crash or performance data related to ads.

Your choices

  • iPhone: the game asks for permission under Apple's App Tracking Transparency before ads load. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your advertising ID is not shared. You can change this anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required: Google's consent form is shown before personalized ads. You can change your choice anytime in More → Ad privacy options.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

If you decline, you may still see ads, but they won't be personalized. Google's use of data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. What we don't do

  • We don't collect your real name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We don't sell or rent personal information.
  • There are no in-app purchases, and nothing in the game can be bought with or exchanged for real money or crypto.

6. Children

The game isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has joined the leaderboard, contact us and we'll delete the entry.

7. Security and retention

Leaderboard data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and kept until you remove it, or until the leaderboard is shut down. Data on your device stays until you delete the app or reset your progress.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

9. Support and contact

Questions, bug reports, a name to report, or a data deletion request? Email yp7buenapark@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.

Tip: if your progress looks wrong, fully close the game and reopen it. Progress is saved every few seconds and whenever you leave the app.

Memecoin Tycoon is a game. Coins are fictional. Not financial advice.
