Common questions

Is any of this real money or crypto?
No. Every coin, price and wallet is made up. Nothing can be bought or cashed out.

How do I turn reminders off?
More → Reminders, or in your phone's Settings.

How do I start over?
More → Reset all progress. This can't be undone.

How do I delete my leaderboard entry?
More → Remove me from the leaderboard. Reset all progress deletes it too.

I lost my streak or progress after deleting the app.
Your save lives on your phone only, so deleting the app deletes it.
